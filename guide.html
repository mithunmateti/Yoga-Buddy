    Where does this come from?

    We found no study that tested the best hold time, so this is mostly our own estimate. We used these as rough guides: the 10–30 seconds in the Common Yoga Protocol (it has no age groups and doesn't cover every pose here), child yoga guidelines, and one-leg balance averages by age, which aren't yoga recommendations. We found no reason for gender to change it. Keep breathing normally, and stop if you feel pain or dizziness.

    • Common Yoga Protocol (Ministry of AYUSH)
    • One-leg balance by age (Springer et al., 2007)
    • Guidelines for Child Yoga (Vikaspedia; we only saw excerpts)

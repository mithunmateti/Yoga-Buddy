📺 Show Yoga Buddy on a TV

Use your phone's own screen mirroring. The TV and the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.

iPhone or iPad Swipe down from the top-right corner → Screen Mirroring → choose your Apple TV or AirPlay TV.
Android Swipe down from the top twice → Cast, Smart View or Screen Cast → choose your TV.
Laptop (Chrome) Click ⋮ at the top-right → Cast, save and share → Cast… → choose your TV.

🎥 Your practice video

Ready?

With recording, tap Done at the end and the video is saved to your phone.
